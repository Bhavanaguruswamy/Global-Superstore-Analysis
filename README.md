# 📊 Global Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the Global Superstore dataset to uncover key business insights related to sales, profit, customer behavior, and regional performance. The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit trends across regions and categories  
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products  
- Understand customer purchasing patterns  
- Build interactive dashboards for business insights  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)  
- SQL  
- Power BI  
- Excel  

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Global Superstore dataset containing ~50,000+ records  
- Includes Orders, Customers, Products, Sales, Profit, and Regions  

---

## 🔍 Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Removed missing and duplicate values  
- Standardized column names and formats  
- Converted date fields for time-based analysis  
- Ensured data consistency across all tables  

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Analyzed sales and profit distribution across regions  
- Examined category-wise and sub-category performance  
- Identified top customers and high-value orders  
- Studied monthly and yearly sales trends  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Technology category generated the highest profit compared to other categories  
- Furniture category showed lower profitability due to higher discounts  
- Top 5 countries contributed a major portion of total revenue  
- Certain regions showed consistent losses, indicating operational inefficiencies  
- Sales peaked during specific months, showing seasonal trends  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Reduce discounts on low-profit or loss-making products  
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing regions and customer segments  
- Optimize inventory for high-demand products  
- Improve strategies for underperforming regions  
- Leverage seasonal trends for targeted campaigns  

---
